K4 continuous-time torque certification — cuRobo TimeOpt (Panda)

Continuous-time inverse-dynamics torque certificates for real cuRobo TimeOpt outputs against the frozen cuRobo Panda rigid-body model (contract v2). Contracts are kept separate: MODEL_K3 (kinematics), MODEL_K4 (model torque vs physical effort limits 87/12 N·m), K3_PLUS_K4, and PLANNING_K4 (cuRobo's own 0.95-scaled torque limits). Physical fidelity of the cuRobo model is NOT established (inconsistent inertia data); the identified Panda model (Gaz et al. 2019) is certified as a separate contract; 3/5 kg payloads are preregistered hypothetical cylinders (not measured); native FP32 torque is BLOCKED_NATIVE_TORQUE. Sampled values are diagnostics; bands are rigorous.
