Research interpretation

This is an exploratory outcome analysis, not a trained or deployable margin-selection policy. Comparing durations only on dual-success cases can produce selection bias when success rates differ. The 0.95 baseline uses the original pinned TimeOpt configuration; other margins are runtime-only overrides. Complete native time inversion and physical-time derivative equivalence remain separate correctness gates.
